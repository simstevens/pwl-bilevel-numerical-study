    Fields¶

    • instance-manifest.jsonl: one line per instance with its name (id), the BOBILib source (source_id), q, p (pieces), the number of regional assignments, the breakpoint mode, the follower variables with a PWL term (selected_variables), their breakpoint offsets (deltas), the random seed, and the SHA-256 checksums of the MPS and AUX files.
    • overview: per method and source-instance size (all for every size), the number of tasks, proved tasks, proof rate, tasks stopped at the limit with and without an incumbent, failed and infeasible tasks, and the median and maximum solver time of the proved tasks in seconds.
